## ✨ Overview
The **Family Tree** project, also known as **Family Connection**, is a private social networking site designed for families.  
It allows members to share pictures and videos, have discussions, and build a family tree together in a secure environment.

---

## ⚙️ How It Works
The system gives administrators full control over access, appearance, and available features.  
Family members can interact through posts, media sharing, and collaborative tools while admins manage the backend.

---

## 🚀 Key Features
- **[Family News](ca://s?q=Explain_Family_News_in_Family_Tree)** – blog for updates and announcements.  
- **[Message Board](ca://s?q=Explain_Message_Board_in_Family_Tree)** – discussions and conversations.  
- **[Address Book](ca://s?q=Explain_Address_Book_in_Family_Tree)** – store family contact information.  
- **[Photo Gallery](ca://s?q=Explain_Photo_Gallery_in_Family_Tree)** – upload and share pictures.  
- **[Video Gallery](ca://s?q=Explain_Video_Gallery_in_Family_Tree)** – share family videos.  
- **[Family Tree](ca://s?q=Explain_Family_Tree_Feature)** – visualize and build family lineage.  
- **[Documents](ca://s?q=Explain_Documents_in_Family_Tree)** – store important family files.  
- **[Chat Room](ca://s?q=Explain_Chat_Room_in_Family_Tree)** – real‑time communication.  
- **[Calendar](ca://s?q=Explain_Calendar_in_Family_Tree)** – track events and milestones.  
- **[Recipes](ca://s?q=Explain_Recipes_in_Family_Tree)** – share family cooking traditions.

## 👩‍💼 Management Features

### General
- Administrators log in to manage settings, database, and configuration.  

### Public/User Facing
- Family members can create accounts, chat, upload media, and interact socially.  

### Admin Facing
- Backup and restore system data.  
- Database management.  
- Settings and server configurations.
